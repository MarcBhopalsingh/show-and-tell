Designed to reduce the friction to capture ideas, reminders and action items while working in the terminal. I designed Plannet because I didn't like having to switch apps or pick up my phone to capture a thought.

In my opinion, tools such as Jira, Notion etc. are too cumbersome for me to use effectively. Plannet allows me
to have visibility of my progress within the terminal itself.

## What it does

- CLI/TUI tool for writing things down.
- Items are tracked in a json file, and can be committed as part of a Git repository.
