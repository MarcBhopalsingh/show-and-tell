Show & Tell is the portfolio you are reading right now. The goal was a site that
gets out of the way: fast, quiet, and trivial to update.

## Principles

- **Content first.** Projects and posts are markdown files validated by a schema.
- **No build-time surprises.** Static output, no client JavaScript by default.
- **Small surface area.** Plain CSS with design tokens, a handful of components.

## Stack

Astro for routing and content collections, TypeScript for type-safe frontmatter,
and a single global stylesheet for the design system. That's the whole thing.
