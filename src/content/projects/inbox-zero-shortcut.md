I kept losing the first hour of my day to email. This Shortcut fixes that.

## The flow

1. Fetch unread mail since the last run
2. Filter out newsletters and receipts with a few simple rules
3. Group the rest by sender importance
4. Offer one-tap draft replies for the common cases

## Why a Shortcut

I wanted something I could run from the lock screen without opening an app. It
runs on a personal automation trigger at 8am and leaves a single notification:
"3 emails need you." Everything else waits.
