---
title: In praise of small automations
description: The best automations aren't the impressive ones. They're the boring ones that quietly remove a daily papercut.
date: 2026-03-10
tags: ["Automation", "Productivity"]
---

People love to show off elaborate automations: the home dashboard, the
twelve-step deploy pipeline, the bot that does everything. I love a different
kind — the one-line Shortcut that saves me four seconds, fifty times a day.

## The math is unintuitive

A task that takes ten seconds, done five times a day, is fifteen hours a year. We
systematically underrate the boring, frequent stuff because each instance feels
too small to bother with.

## My rule

If I notice myself doing the same fiddly thing twice in a week, it goes on a list.
Once a month I automate the top three. No grand system — just steady removal of
papercuts.
