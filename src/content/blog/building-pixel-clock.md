The hardware was the easy part. A Pi, a panel, a power supply that could actually
deliver the amps. The hard part — the part that took weeks — was making it feel
like an object instead of a project.

## The 80/20 of "finished"

Getting pixels on the screen took an afternoon. Making it something I wanted on
my desk took much longer:

- A diffuser so individual LEDs blur into clean glyphs
- Auto-brightness so it isn't blinding at night
- A graceful boot animation instead of a flash of garbage

None of these are technically interesting. All of them are why I still use it.

## What I'd do differently

Next time I'd design the enclosure first. I retrofitted a case around finished
electronics, and it shows in a few millimeters of awkward gaps.
